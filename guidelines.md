# General F.A.R.T.S. Guidelines

## Participant Requirements

### Driver Requirements

- BeamNG.drive - Soft-body physics driving simulator 
  - https://www.beamng.com/game/
- Method for controlling their racecar:
  - Racing wheel (strongly recommended)
  - Game controller
  - Keyboard (highly discouraged, but allowed)

### Constructors Requirements

- Automation - The Car Company Tycoon Game
   https://www.automationgame.com/home/
- Constructors must also participate as a driver of their racecar

## Season Guidelines

- All constructors and drivers must join at the start of the season
- Cars must be designed within the regulations for the season
- Regulations will determine car body type, engine details, and limitations
- Cars must be designed only with technology available for that year (by setting the year appropriately in Automation)
- Screenshots of car overview page must be posted for review
- All teams must sign off on all cars to ensure cars are within regulations
- Cars must be submitted and distributed to all drivers ahead of time (BeamMP requirement)
- Cars can be modified mid-season, but with a grid penalty for the next race

### Mid-Season Car Modifications

- All body modifications must be done as a new trim level of the original car
- All engine modifications must be done as a new variant of the original engine
- All changes must still be done within the technology available for that year
- Changes must be submitted and reapproved prior to the next race

## Race Guidelines

- All races will be done in BeamNG.drive with the BeamMP mod, with cars exported from Automation
- Practice time will be allowed prior to qualifying, with all teams on track
- A qualifying period will then take place, where teams will attempt fastest time
- The lineup will then be determined by qualifying times
- The game will then be reloaded for race time, where teams will line up
- Racing will then start for a predetermined number of laps
- Cars can be repaired, but only when fully stopped in the pit lane
- Flipped cars can be repaired on-track, but then must pit for a 10-second stop-go penalty

### Scoring

1st - 10 points
2nd -  6 points
3rd -  4 points
4th -  3 points
5th -  2 points
6th -  1 point
DQ  - No points
